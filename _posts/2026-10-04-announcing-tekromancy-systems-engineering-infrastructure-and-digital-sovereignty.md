For years, this personal blog has served as a workbench and digital notebook for open-source experiments, custom shell tools, penetration testing setups, Docker recipes, and Linux kernel adventures.

As these projects grew, it became clear that there was a need for a broader collaborative platform: a dedicated space where fellow systems engineers, infrastructure hackers, and open-source practitioners can pool their expertise to publish deep-dive guides, field reports, sovereign tools, and architectural breakdowns.

Today, I'm excited to share [**Tekromancy.com**](https://tekromancy.com/), a collaborative publication focused on infrastructure engineering, kernel internals, Kubernetes orchestration, AI/ML systems, and cybersecurity.

---

### What is Tekromancy?

The name *Tekromancy* represents technology indistinguishable from magic—grounded in deep open-source understanding, digital sovereignty, and battle-tested systems craftsmanship.

Rather than high-level tech news or surface-level summaries, [Tekromancy](https://tekromancy.com/) is committed to substantive, code-level engineering content across five core pillars:

1. **Linux & Kernel Internals**: Deep dives into sysfs, eBPF tracing, memory management, systemd services, custom kernels, and minimal Wayland compositors (like Hyprland).
2. **Kubernetes & Cloud Native**: Bare-metal clusters, GitOps deployment pipelines, edge orchestration, persistent storage fabrics, and ingress security.
3. **Cybersecurity & SecOps**: Offensive security techniques, hardened network tunnels (WireGuard), credential sandboxing, privilege boundaries, and penetration testing field ops.
4. **AI / ML Ops & Engineering**: Running local LLM inference engines, sovereign hardware acceleration, batch data pipelines, and reproducible training setups.
5. **Advanced Networking**: Distributed mesh topologies, DNS architecture, multi-homed BGP routing, and private overlay networks.

---

### Beyond Just Blog Posts: Sovereign Apps & Field Ops

In addition to technical articles and architectural tutorials, [Tekromancy.com](https://tekromancy.com/) hosts:

- **Sovereign Web Apps**: Standalone, client-side tools and utilities built to run in the browser without creepy trackers or third-party cloud lock-in.
- **Field Ops & Community Events**: Guides and dispatches from real-world deployments, conferences, local tech meetups, and cybersecurity capture-the-flag events.
- **Open Standards**: Fully syndicated via [RSS Feed](https://tekromancy.com/rss.xml) and crafted with lightweight modern web stacks (Astro & modern CSS) for instant page loads.
